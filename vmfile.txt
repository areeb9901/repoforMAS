We have reviewed the issue encountered while changing the Agreement Type for MA 31176 from ISDA 1992 to ISDA 2002.

The error occurs because MA 31176 is currently linked to a Synthetic Repo Master Agreement through its "masterNettingId". As per the existing business validation, only specific agreement types are allowed to be linked to a Synthetic Repo Master Agreement.

This validation and the corresponding error message were introduced as part of BEADEV-12748.

Therefore, in order to change the Agreement Type of MA 31176 to ISDA 2002, the MA would first need to be unlinked from the Synthetic Repo Master Agreement. Once the linkage is removed, the Agreement Type change can be performed.
